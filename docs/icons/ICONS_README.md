# Icons and brand assets

The LeadsGhr logo is the **Growth** mark: three rising bars (the first one lighter) with a trend line above them, next to the word **Leads**Ghr, in charcoal and green (chosen 2026-11-20, in use since 2026-11-22). It replaced the earlier purple LG monogram, whose files were removed.

- **Inside the desktop app** the logo is not an image. `BrandLogo` (mark and wordmark) and `BrandMark` (mark only) in `packages/ui/src/brand-logo.tsx` draw it from the design tokens, so it follows the light and dark themes: the bars and "Leads" use `--ui-logo-bar` (the text colour), the short bar uses `--ui-logo-bar-soft`, and the trend line and "Ghr" use `--ui-color-primary`. The sidebar (full and collapsed), the Dashboard header, and the splash screen use them.
- **License key illustration:** the key on the license screen is `LicenseKeyArt` in `packages/ui/src/license-key-art.tsx`, drawn from the tokens (outline in the text colour, body in the surface colour, shading in the brand colour), so it follows the theme. It replaced `public/leadsghr-license-key.svg/.png`; the license screens show the Mailer and Tracker marks with `MailerMark` and `TrackerMark` instead of the removed `public/leadsghr-mailer-icon.svg` and `public/leadsghr-tracker-icon.svg`.
- **Image files** for places outside the app, in `public/`: `leadsghr-logo.svg` / `.png` (for light backgrounds), `leadsghr-logo-dark.svg` / `.png` (for dark backgrounds), `leadsghr-icon.svg` and `leadsghr-icon-512.png` (mark only, light backgrounds), `leadsghr-icon-light.svg` (mark only, dark backgrounds), and `leadsghr-app-icon.svg` with PNGs from 16 to 1024 px (the mark on a charcoal rounded tile). The SVG wordmarks name Plus Jakarta Sans, so use the PNGs where that font is not installed.
- **Desktop icons:** `node scripts/regenerate-brand-icons.mjs` renders `public/leadsghr-app-icon.svg` into `apps/desktop/resources/leadsghr-icon.png` (1024 × 1024, window and taskbar) and `leadsghr-icon.ico` (16 to 256 px, installer, setup, and uninstall programs).

The LeadsGhr Mailer extension has its own mark: a faceted paper plane in the design-token colours, with the upper wing in the brand green gradient (`--ui-brand-accent` to `--ui-brand-primary` to `--ui-brand-primary-hover`) and the lower wing in the ink colour (`--ui-brand-ink`), separated by a white gap. `node scripts/regenerate-extension-icons.mjs` writes it from `tokens.css` (light theme) to `apps/mailer-extension/public/icon.svg`, its 128 × 128 transparent render `icon.png` (toolbar, popup header, settings page), and `apps/mailer-extension/email-assets/leadsghr-mailer-logo.png`, the image shown in outgoing emails; re-run it when the brand colours change. The email image must then be uploaded to the Supabase `assets` bucket (`email-assets/`) by the owner, because uploading is a live service change. The LeadsGhr Tracker mark is a double tick on the charcoal tile, like the LeadsGhr app icon: "sent" in white and "opened" in green (`--ui-brand-accent`); the same script writes `apps/extension/public/icon.svg` and `icon.png`. Full-colour, theme-aware versions of both marks are available as `MailerMark` and `TrackerMark` in `packages/ui/src/brand-logo.tsx` (in the dark theme the Tracker tile turns light and its "opened" tick uses `--ui-logo-accent`); the sidebar uses the duotone line versions instead.

**Sidebar icons** use the duotone line style (chosen 2026-11-22): `NavIcon` in `packages/ui/src/nav-icons.tsx` draws each as an outline in the menu text colour with one accent part in `--ui-color-primary`, so they follow the theme and turn with the text on the open item. Dashboard (one square green), Pipeline (last node green), Crawler (globe with a green centre), New Search (magnifier with a green plus), Niche Library (books with a green bookmark), LeadsGhr Scraper (robot head with green eyes), Email Filtering (funnel with a green plus), Email Validation (envelope with a green check), Email Spam Check (octagon with a green exclamation mark), Email Sending (the Mailer plane, upper wing green), Email Templates (a page with a green line), Email Tracking (the Tracker double tick, "opened" tick green), Leads (person with a green second person), Runs (clock with green hands), Profiles (document with green lines), Exports (tray with a green arrow), Compliance (shield with a green check), and Settings (gear with a green centre). See AGENTS.md, "Sidebar icons and adding a sidebar tab". Other interface icons are rounded outline SVGs on a 24 px grid in `packages/ui/src/primitives.tsx`. The Navigation, metrics, actions, and status controls use text or accessible labels with icons.

**LeadsGhr Scraper icon** (chosen 2026-11-24, option R2; replaces the gear-and-globe W1): a robot head. The extension's toolbar icon (`apps/scraper-extension/public/icon.svg`/`icon.png`, generated from the tokens by `scripts/regenerate-extension-icons.mjs`) is a charcoal tile like the Tracker's with a solid white head, ears, and antenna, green eyes and antenna light, and a charcoal mouth. `ScraperMark` in `packages/ui/src/brand-logo.tsx` draws the same icon from the tokens for Settings → License, beside `MailerMark` and `TrackerMark`. `NavIcon name="scraper"` (sidebar tab, New Search's **Send to LeadsGhr Scraper** button, Scraper Results) is the duotone line version: the head in outline with filled green eyes and antenna light.

The Profile character avatar is generated by `react-nice-avatar`. Its seed is stored in local browser storage on first creation and replaced only when the user presses **Generate new**. It is not a brand logo.

The Gmail tracking extension, **LeadsGhr Tracker**, has its own mark: a rounded square with a double tick cut out of it (the background shows through), filled with one gradient that runs through both LeadsGhr Mailer plane colours, from `#6E0CF2` and `#6403E8` at the top right through `#3B0197` to deep purple `#1B003A` at the bottom left. The source is `apps/extension/public/icon.svg` (also used for the Gmail top-bar control); `apps/extension/public/icon.png` is its 128 × 128 transparent render for the toolbar, popup header, settings page, and notifications. The desktop app keeps the LG monogram.

The LeadsGhr mark is an angular purple and deep-purple LG monogram on a transparent background. The light-background wordmark uses dark-purple `Leads` and purple `Ghr`; the sidebar variant uses white `Leads` and light-violet `Ghr`. Navigation icons are white on the dark rail; dashboard metric icons use purple strokes.
